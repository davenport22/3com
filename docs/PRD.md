## 1. Problem

What does a user fail to do today, and what does that cost them?
One paragraph, no solution language.

## 2. Target user

Who exactly. Not "fitness enthusiasts". Name the segment, their current tool,
and why they would switch.

## 3. What 3com is

One sentence a stranger understands. Then three bullets on what it does.

Note: the name suggests three of something. If that is the product hook
(three disciplines, three pillars, three-day cycles), say so here, because it
drives the whole information architecture.

## 4. Core user journey

The single loop the product lives or dies on, step by step.
Example shape: open app, start activity, record, stop, see result, come back
tomorrow. Write yours.

## 6. Data

- What gets recorded, at what granularity?
- What comes from the user, what from device sensors, what from third-party
  imports (Apple Health, Health Connect, Garmin, Strava)?
- What does the user see back, in which views?

## 7. Social and sharing

Is there a social layer at all? Friends, groups, leaderboards, public
profiles, shareable activity cards? This decision changes the backend
security model, so it cannot be deferred.

## 8. Business model

Free, freemium, subscription, one-off. If paid, what is behind the paywall
and when does billing need to exist?

## 9. Success metrics

What number tells us this works after 4 weeks and after 6 months?

## 10. Constraints

Deadline, budget, team size, launch market, required languages, store
requirements.
